*   **Dip:** The shooting process begins with a "dip," where the ball is brought down to waist level. During this phase, Curry aligns the ball, his right knee, and his right eye in a vertical line to ensure the ball travels in a straight path toward the basket.
*   **Ball Path:** Curry employs a **"Two-Handed Delivery."** While he is a one-handed shooter at the point of release, he uses both hands to lift the ball from the waist to the head level. This technique, developed during his youth to compensate for a lack of strength, provides significant power and distance.
*   **Elbow and Wrist Action:** The Turn Stance reduces the gap between his eye and his elbow. This allows him to keep his elbow tucked without creating unnecessary tension in his shoulders or biceps, facilitating a more fluid motion.
*   **Release Height/Timing:** He is noted for an exceptionally quick release. The transition from the two-handed delivery to the one-handed release occurs near the head level.
*   **Lower-Body Drive:** His shooting motion is highly integrated with his lower body, using the momentum from his legs to drive the ball, especially on long-distance shots.
*   **Follow-Through:** The release is performed with the right hand, maintaining a consistent follow-through while the left hand acts as a guide during the delivery phase.
