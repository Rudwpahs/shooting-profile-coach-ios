# Fluid Motion Player Review

The Motion Studio player now renders continuous display poses between the five audited source phases. The source phases remain marker positions on a single timeline; they are not presented as five separate film cards. At marker endpoints, the renderer returns the original stored joint coordinates exactly. Between markers, it uses bounded smooth interpolation for display only.

The hydrated `390 × 844` mobile capture confirms the revised player keeps the skeleton on a dark central stage, retains orange shooting-arm emphasis, replaces the large zoom bar with pinch/drag plus a small reset control, and places phase names with source-time markers under the stage. The Motion Studio card stack below the viewer has been reduced to one compact analysis note.
